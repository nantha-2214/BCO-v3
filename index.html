Contacts

    Scan a new card

    Take photo opens the camera. Attach file picks a saved photo, screenshot or image file. Lay the card on a plain surface; the app cuts away everything around the card.
    Most accurate: use your phone's text scanner

    iPhone

    1. Tap the box below.
    2. Tap Scan Text on the bar above the keyboard (or touch and hold the box, then tap Scan Text).
    3. Point at the card, wait for the text to frame, tap Insert.

    Already took the photo? Open it in Photos, touch and hold the text, Select All, Copy, then paste here.

    Android

    1. Open the card photo in Google Photos and tap Lens (or open the Google app and tap the camera icon in the search bar, then point at the card).
    2. Tap Text, then Select all, then Copy.
    3. Come back here, touch and hold the box below, tap Paste.

    Samsung: in Gallery, tap the T icon on the photo, Select all, Copy, then paste here.

    Something wrong? Choose a field, then tap the correct line.
